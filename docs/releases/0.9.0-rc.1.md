# Cerea Vice 0.9.0-rc.1

Release candidate for Windows x64, covering the 0.9 free-driving and fare-board
milestone: the follow-ups to #11 (fares on demand, chosen from a list) and #7
(passengers walk to the taxi instead of waiting at every stop). It is a
portable playtest build; owner gameplay and physical controller testing are
still pending.

## Play

Download the Windows ZIP, extract the entire folder to a local Windows drive,
and open **cerea-vice.exe**. Keep all the extracted files together. Node.js,
npm, a server and an internet connection are not needed to play.

The briefing offers **Cerea Centro**, **Harbor District**, and **Cerea outskirts
prototype**. The outskirts district connects real Cerea to a clearly labelled
fictional test hamlet. Choose the district before starting.

This development build is unsigned and has no installer or automatic updater.
Windows may display an unknown-publisher prompt. Check the supplied SHA-256
checksums before running a downloaded copy.

## Changes since 0.8.0-rc.3

- **Free driving.** A shift starts, and every dropoff ends, with you free to
  drive. No fare pops up or is assigned automatically. Free driving uses the
  normal 07:00–17:00 shift clock; pause, atlas and the fare list still freeze it.
- **Request fares** at a full stop with **D-pad down / O** (both remappable in
  **Change controls**, with conflict checks, saved bindings and Restore
  defaults). Help, the dashboard prompt and hints show your chosen binding.
  Existing 0.8 control profiles keep their choices and gain a free key.
- **Fare list.** Up to four distinct fares, each with passenger, pickup,
  destination and the complete guaranteed payment, including any long
  collection supplement, plus the maximum speed bonus. Up / down selects,
  **A / Enter** accepts, **B / Backspace** closes without accepting, **Y / N**
  replaces the whole list without a penalty. Reading it pauses the shift, and it
  ignores presses still held from driving.
- **A stable list.** Closing, reopening, pausing and saving/loading keep the
  same fares and quotes. The list is replaced only when you ask for a new one;
  accepting a fare removes the rest, so the next request after a dropoff issues
  a fresh list. A closed list never reopens by itself.
- **Passengers arrive and board.** Taxi stops no longer show waiting people.
  After stopped passenger service at the accepted pickup, the passenger walks
  from a nearby doorway or off-street spot to the taxi (around the bonnet or
  boot when needed) and sits beside you. The taxi is held for those few
  seconds; the speed-bonus clock starts when they are seated. At dropoff the
  seat clears and they walk away.
- **Information while free driving** says where you are without suggesting a
  destination.

Fare amounts, the speed bonus and payout rules are unchanged. The compass still
shows car heading. There is no GPS, minimap, route line, objective arrow or
live atlas marker, and a walking passenger appears only beside your stopped
taxi. Requesting, accepting, boarding, dropoff and saving never move the atlas
marker; information requests and deliberate estimates remain the only ways to
change it.

## Saves and upgrades

Windows player data is in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CereaVice\profile`. Both portable
copies and the WSL playtest launcher use this location. Closing the game or
replacing its application folder keeps saves and control/presentation settings.
Close the old copy before launching an upgrade, and keep the player profile.

Saves are now version 2: they add free driving, the fare list with its fare
identities and quotes, and the progress of a passenger walking to the taxi.
Saving during that walk resumes it from the same point. A departing passenger
after dropoff is not saved. **0.8 saves still load**: a pending offer becomes a
one-fare list with the same passenger, journey and quote (an unquoted one is
quoted at your next stopped request), and an accepted ride stays accepted.
Damaged, newer or changed-map saves are reported and the stored save is kept.

## Playtest focus

Whether free driving plus on-demand fares feels better than automatic offers;
the list's readability and D-pad down / O ergonomics on a physical controller;
whether four fares are the right number; the length and readability of the
boarding walk (about five seconds, brisk for the larger bays), including
looking right to watch it; and Save → close → relaunch → Continue on Windows,
also during a boarding walk.

Not verified in this candidate: physical controller ergonomics and a full
Windows close/relaunch gameplay check remain owner checks. Controller
automation simulates a standard gamepad only. Walking passengers avoid
building footprints, water and the taxi body, but not decorative props such as
trees or lamps.

The build is prepared from the `task/free-driving-fare-board` branch for an
independent review. It has not been merged. Issues #7 and #11 remain open for
owner playtesting.

file hashes. `SHA256SUMS.txt` covers the ZIP, release information and these notes.
