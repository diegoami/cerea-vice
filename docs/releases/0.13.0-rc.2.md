# Cerea Vice 0.13.0-rc.2

Release candidate for Windows x64: **Verona centro**, a new district inside the Adige loop, the town
doctor that checks every map, and a fix for a Legnago stop that could not be served. It replaces
0.13.0-rc.1, which was not published: its milestone review (#79) found every church labelled San Zeno on
the atlas and on direction boards outside Cerea (#80), and a build-tool option that could delete files
(#81); both are fixed here. It is a portable playtest build; owner gameplay, Windows performance and
physical controller checks are still pending.

## Play

Download the Windows ZIP, extract the entire folder to a local Windows drive, and open
**cerea-vice.exe**. Keep all the extracted files together. Node.js, npm, a server and an internet
connection are not needed to play.

This development build is unsigned and has no installer or automatic updater. Windows may display an
unknown-publisher prompt. Check the supplied SHA-256 checksums before running a downloaded copy.

## Changes since 0.12.0-rc.2

### Verona centro

- A new district in the briefing: the centre of Verona inside the Adige loop, about 1.9 km², from the
  Arena and Piazza Bra to the Duomo and Castelvecchio, with 417 real streets, all drivable, and the
  river with its bridges.
- **13 stops:** Arena, Palazzo Barbieri (the shift starts here), Teatro Filarmonico, San Lorenzo, Porta
  Borsari, Piazza delle Erbe, Casa di Giulietta, Porta Leoni, San Fermo Maggiore, Biblioteca Civica,
  Duomo, Sant'Anastasia and Castelvecchio.
- **Squares** such as Piazza Bra, Piazza delle Erbe and Piazza dei Signori are paved open ground you can
  drive across and stop in. Directions and fares still follow the streets through them.
- Courtyards are kept (Palazzo Barbieri), and Torre dei Lamberti stands at its real 84 m.
- The atlas now draws rivers and squares as areas.

### Fixes

- **Legnago's Chiesa dell'Assunta stop** (Lower Veronese plain) could not be served: its bay was walled in
  by buildings, so the taxi could not get into it. The bay now sits beside Corso della Vittoria (#73).
- **Place names on the atlas and on direction boards:** outside Cerea, every church was labelled
  "San Zeno", and libraries and town halls "Biblioteca" and "Municipio", on the atlas map and on the
  direction boards of the Lower Veronese plain. Every place now shows its own name; Cerea keeps its
  familiar short forms (#80).
- A river drawn both as a line and as an area no longer blocks a bridge over it.

### Street-name plates

- Plate textures use a quarter of the memory they did, and the streamed maps build plates only within
  300 m of the taxi. Plates may look slightly softer right up close.

### For map builders

- **Town doctor:** `npm run town:check -- [district]` checks a map for a connected road network, stops
  reachable both ways, bays the taxi can get into, buildings clear of roads, every road drivable, street
  plates on their own street, and size against budgets. The tests run it on every listed district.
- **Detail presets:** a town spec can ask for `sketch` (fewer, merged buildings, plates on main roads
  only, no props) or `full` (smaller footprints kept, more façade variety) as well as `town`.
- Town specs can turn on squares and building courtyards, and ask for tiled delivery. A tiled build only
  writes `<folder>/manifest.json` and its `tiles/`, never anything else in the folder (#81).

There is still no GPS, minimap, route line, objective arrow or live atlas marker.

## Saves and settings

Windows player data is in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CereaVice\profile`.

- **Lower Veronese plain saves from 0.12 no longer load** (map data version 3, since the Assunta bay
  moved). The game names both versions and keeps the old save; start a new shift there.
- Cerea Centro, outskirts and Harbor saves from 0.12 still load. Settings and control profiles are kept.

## Playtest focus

- Verona: does driving the centre feel right with every street open, are the squares and stops easy to
  find, and is it smooth on Windows (3,554 buildings, 48 tiles)?
- Street-name plates: still readable at the distances you read them from?
- Legnago: a fare to or from Chiesa dell'Assunta.
- Direction boards with long place names (for example Chiesa dei Santi Biagio, Fermo e Rustico in
  Casaleone): still readable, or too squeezed?

Not verified in this candidate: Windows performance and memory (measured only under a software renderer
on Linux), the WSL launcher on the Windows host, and physical controllers.
