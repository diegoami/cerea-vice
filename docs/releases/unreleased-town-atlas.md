# Unreleased: town atlas and destination signs

Continuous-service maps now open a detailed map of the current town, with a lightweight
regional view showing towns and connecting roads. Zoom responds faster, and dragging
past a town edge continues across the region. Paper navigation freezes the car/time
and keeps the last information/estimate marker. The 3D background and mirrors stop
redrawing while the atlas is open, with resize and return-to-drive redraws preserved.

Town signs now guide legal road exits to town entrances. Landmark direction boards
include local brown turn signs and grouped names for co-located civic destinations.
Arrows show actual exit angles; street names remain on their own plates. Guidance is
static across fares and uses the same directed road rules as gameplay.

Map content, signatures and save formats are unchanged. Legacy fixture atlases retain
their original page behavior. No release tag or publication is created by this change.
See [implementation and checks](../ATLAS_AND_GUIDANCE.md).
