# Cerea Vice 0.11.0-rc.1

Release candidate for Windows x64, covering milestone 0.11 (the towns pipeline and the first region). It is
a portable playtest build; owner gameplay, Windows performance and physical controller checks are still
pending.

## Play

Download the Windows ZIP, extract the entire folder to a local Windows drive, and open
**cerea-vice.exe**. Keep all the extracted files together. Node.js, npm, a server and an internet
connection are not needed to play.

The briefing now lists **Lower Veronese plain** alongside **Cerea Centro**, **Harbor District** and the
**Cerea outskirts prototype**.

This development build is unsigned and has no installer or automatic updater. Windows may display an
unknown-publisher prompt. Check the supplied SHA-256 checksums before running a downloaded copy.

## Changes since 0.10.0-rc.1

### A region of six real towns

- **Lower Veronese plain**: Cerea, Legnago, Bovolone, Sanguinetto, Nogara and Casaleone, each with its real
  streets at real scale, joined by their real main roads. The roads between towns keep their junctions,
  one-way rules and names, but are shortened so neighbouring towns are a few minutes apart: at about
  60 km/h, Cerea–Casaleone is 2 minutes, Cerea–Sanguinetto 4, Cerea–Legnago 5, Cerea–Bovolone 7, and the
  far ends (Legnago–Nogara) 14.
- **40 stops** across the six towns, as confirmed by the owner, with fares between any two of them. Stop
  names that do not already name their town show it, for example "Poste Italiane (Bovolone)".
- **Direction boards** where a road leaves a town give the next town and its **real** road distance, for
  example "LEGNAGO 8 km"; the drive is shorter than the sign says, as the roads between towns are
  shortened.
- **Rivers as areas**: the Adige at Legnago is drawn as water and is solid, while its bridges stay
  drivable.
- The district is streamed in 250 m tiles; the HUD and atlas header say "REAL TOWNS / SHORTENED ROADS".
- The villages between the towns are not in this candidate yet (#50); the roads pass through countryside
  there.

### Driving

- **Top speed** in Picture & Sound: **No limit** (default) lets full throttle keep gaining on paved roads;
  **Normal (72 km/h)** restores the old limit. Acceleration below about 54 km/h is unchanged, part
  throttle settles at a steady speed, and the car moves in half-metre steps so it cannot pass through
  walls. Reverse and grass keep their limits; leaving the road fast brakes hard.

### Cerea's buildings

- About 40% of Cerea's buildings were missing one corner (some four-sided buildings were triangles) in
  drawing, collision and the atlas. All footprints are now complete.

### Districts and data

- The district list comes from a registry; a district whose map is missing or damaged offers the others
  instead of stopping the game.
- Map data notice: `maps/NOTICE.md` in the bundle states the OpenStreetMap source and ODbL 1.0 terms
  (`docs/LICENSING.md`).

There is still no GPS, minimap, route line, objective arrow or live atlas marker. The compass shows car
heading; information requests and deliberate estimates remain the only ways to move the atlas marker.

## Saves and settings

Windows player data is in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CereaVice\profile`.

- **Cerea Centro and Cerea outskirts saves from earlier versions no longer load**, because the building
  fix changed those maps (map data version 2). The game says so, names both versions, and keeps the old
  save; start a new shift. Harbor saves still load.
- Settings and control profiles are kept; the new Top speed setting starts at No limit.

## Playtest focus

- The **region**: load time, smoothness and memory on your Windows machine; whether the drives between
  towns feel right (about 5 minutes for neighbours); whether the towns feel like themselves; the
  direction boards; fares between towns; the Adige and its bridges at Legnago.
- **No top speed**: how it feels at high speed with keyboard and controller.
- **Cerea's buildings** after the corner fix.

Not verified in this candidate: Windows load time, memory and frame time for the region (all measurements
used a software renderer under Linux), physical controllers, and listening. Controller automation
simulates a standard gamepad only.
