# Cerea Vice 0.10.0-rc.1

Release candidate for Windows x64, covering milestone 0.10 (sound, radio, colour, picture quality
and less text) and the 0.9 free-driving and fare-list work, which was never published on its own.
It is a portable playtest build; owner listening, viewing, gameplay and physical controller checks
are still pending.

## Play

Download the Windows ZIP, extract the entire folder to a local Windows drive, and open
**cerea-vice.exe**. Keep all the extracted files together. Node.js, npm, a server and an internet
connection are not needed to play.

The briefing offers **Cerea Centro**, **Harbor District**, and **Cerea outskirts prototype**. The
outskirts district connects real Cerea to a clearly labelled fictional test hamlet.

This development build is unsigned and has no installer or automatic updater. Windows may display an
unknown-publisher prompt. Check the supplied SHA-256 checksums before running a downloaded copy.

## Changes since 0.8.0-rc.3

### Sound and radio (#14, #17)

- **Vehicle sound you can hear.** A new engine whose pitch and brightness follow speed and throttle,
  a gear whine in reverse, a clunk when selecting D or R, brake hiss while moving and road rumble
  that grows with speed. The default volume is now **75%** (Mute, 25, 50, 75 or 100%); a level you
  saved earlier is kept.
- **Sound status on every screen.** The dashboard shows the level, mute, and whether Windows is
  blocking audio (`SOUND BLOCKED · CLICK OR PRESS A KEY`). Changing Volume plays a short check tone.
- **A radio with your own music.** In **Picture & Sound**: Radio on/off (off by default), Radio
  source (built-in tune or **My music folder**), Music level, **Choose music folder…**, Previous
  track and Next track. While driving, **V / D-pad right** skips to the next track (remappable in
  Change controls). Music has its own level, dips slightly under throttle, and pauses in place
  with the game. Mute silences everything.
- **Music folder.** Chosen with the Windows folder dialog and remembered across relaunches.
  Supported: MP3, OGG / Opus, WAV, FLAC, M4A / AAC, WebM, up to three folder levels and 2000 tracks.
  No music is included with the game. A missing, empty or unreadable folder, or an unplayable track,
  gives a short message and the built-in tune plays instead. The game can read only the folder you
  chose, and only its audio files.

### Colour and picture quality (#15, #16)

- **A less green town.** The olive cast came mainly from grey-green materials under warm light, and
  the retro modes amplified it. Façades are now ochre, cream, terracotta and grey stucco with
  varied shutters; roads are neutral, stopping bays cool grey, the town ground greige; the
  countryside mixes meadow, stubble and a green crop field; the sun is less yellow and the haze
  grey-blue. Street plates, signs and boards keep their colours and contrast.
- **Picture options from lightest to heaviest:** Retro 270p, Retro 540p (still the default), Sharp
  and the new opt-in **High** (up to 3840×2160 at pixel ratio 2). A line under the choice explains
  each mode and shows the size actually drawn. Pressing A on the controller now cycles in this
  order. The game never switches to High by itself.

### Less text (#19)

Flavour and repeated text was removed from the briefing, HUD, notices, atlas, pause, results and
fare list. Controls and prompts (with your bindings), fares and pay, street and place names, the
clock, earnings, the compass and every passenger's full destination request are unchanged. The
record of every decision is `docs/TEXT_AUDIT.md`.

### Free driving and the fare list (#11, #7, from 0.9.0-rc.1)

A shift starts, and every dropoff ends, with you free to drive. Stop and press **D-pad down / O** to
see up to four fares with their guaranteed pay; accept one, close the list, or ask for a new one.
At the accepted pickup the passenger walks from a nearby doorway to the taxi and sits beside you.
Details in the [0.9.0-rc.1 notes](0.9.0-rc.1.md).

Fare amounts, the speed bonus and payout rules are unchanged. There is no GPS, minimap, route line,
objective arrow or live atlas marker; sound and music never depend on position or route.

## Saves and settings

Windows player data is in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CereaVice\profile`; the music-folder choice is stored
there too. Map data is unchanged since 0.8.0-rc.3 and saves are still version 2, so **0.8 and 0.9
saves load** (0.8 offers migrate as described in the 0.9 notes). Control profiles from 0.8 and 0.9
keep every custom binding and gain free keys for the new actions. Close the old copy before
launching an upgrade, and keep the player profile.

## Playtest focus

- **Listening on Windows:** is the engine clearly audible at 75% through your speakers or
  headphones, after a keyboard start and a controller-only start, and does it stay pleasant over a
  shift? Is the music level balanced against it?
- **Radio:** choose a folder of your own music (MP3, FLAC, M4A), skip tracks with V / D-pad right,
  relaunch, then rename the folder and relaunch again.
- **Colours:** does the town still feel too green, or now too brown or dusty? Are stopping bays easy
  to spot in grey?
- **High mode:** does it look clearer on your display, and does driving stay smooth?
- **Text:** is anything you relied on missing?
- **From 0.9:** free driving with on-demand fares, the fare list on a physical controller (D-pad
  down / O), the boarding walk, and Save → close → relaunch → Continue.

Not verified in this candidate: audibility and sound quality through real Windows output, real
MP3, FLAC and M4A decoding (only WAV was played in tests), the Windows folder dialog, real HiDPI,
1440p and 4K displays, GPU frame times (measurements used a software renderer), and physical
controller ergonomics. Controller automation simulates a standard gamepad only.
